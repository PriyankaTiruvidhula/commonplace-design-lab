# Commonplace brand and launch concept

Built with Instinct (AI assistance). Fictional brand and proposed campaign only. Candidate review pending. No posts have been sent, no ad spend approved and no results measured.

## Brand story

Good ideas deserve a common place. Commonplace is a fictional learning-workspace concept about moving from scattered knowledge to small, shared actions. Its story follows three verbs: collect, learn, share. The identity is intentionally calm and editorial rather than presenting AI as magic. An assistant interface is part of the concept, but the implemented demo is scripted and does not run a model.

## Visual guidelines

- Wordmark: lowercase `commonplace`, simple sans-serif, with a circular `c.` symbol. Original exercise asset, not a trademark-clearance claim.
- Ink #251F21 for primary content; secondary #585254 for supporting copy.
- Teal #73A89A for selected/known accents; deep green #244C43 for buttons and text requiring stronger contrast.
- White #FFFFFF for content, sand #F4EFEC for quiet support panels, pale green #EAF2EE for illustrative backgrounds.
- Display: Georgia at normal weight, open spacing and short headlines. Text: Arial/Helvetica. System fonts avoid remote font downloads.
- Components: rounded cards, clearly labelled controls, one primary CTA per group, visible focus outline, ample whitespace. Do not use pale teal as small body text on white.
- Voice: warm, specific, people-first. Avoid unverified productivity numbers, customer logos, testimonials, certifications and implied real AI functionality.

## Proposed LinkedIn strategy

Audience: learners and team leads interested in sharing useful knowledge. Objective: start a conversation and invite exploration of a clearly labelled concept. Organic concept posts only, no paid targeting or real publishing. CTA points to a review demo once owner-approved sharing exists. Do not claim sign-ups, active users or performance. If later published, define real metrics before collecting analytics and respect consent/privacy.

## Five proposed posts

### 1. Introduce the problem, individual learners

You do not need a perfect learning plan to begin.

Start with a short guide and one idea you can use. Commonplace is a fictional workspace concept that brings useful resources and a manageable next step together.

What helps you turn something you read into something you try?

Visual: banner 1. CTA: explore the concept when a shareable demo is approved. Not a product launch announcement for a real company.

### 2. A shared-learning habit, team leads

Useful knowledge should not stop at one person.

Try a five-minute check-in: one thing learned, one situation where it might help, one question for the team. A small conversation can be easier to start than another long document.

What makes sharing knowledge feel useful rather than like extra work?

Visual: banner 2. CTA: discuss a habit. No promised business outcome.

### 3. Behind the design, product/design audience

Why does this learning-workspace concept begin with four resources instead of a large dashboard?

The first design hypothesis is focus: a clear title, a topic, a reading time and one save action. These choices still need real user feedback. The current prototype is an exercise, not proof that the design is validated.

What would you test first?

Visual: workspace screenshot. CTA: invite feedback after owner approval.

### 4. Explain the assistant honestly, technology audience

An assistant-shaped interface is not the same as an AI-powered product.

In this Commonplace concept, three sample prompts lead to fixed example replies. That lets us explore layout and interaction without implying that a live model can search documents or understand a team.

Prototype first, explain the limits, then test the real problem.

Visual: scripted assistant screenshot. CTA: discuss transparent prototyping.

### 5. Concept release, mixed audience

Meet Commonplace, a personal design-and-web concept built with AI assistance.

Explore a responsive landing page, a sample learning workspace and a small campaign studio. Search resources, save an idea, try a session-only task and compare audience-specific messages.

It is a fictional brand, not a commercial service. There is no live AI, real customer data or published marketing campaign. Candidate review is still pending.

Visual: landing-page screenshot. CTA: review a demo only when sharing is approved.
